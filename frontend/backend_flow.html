Deep Dive: BirdNET

1

The Science

Developed by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and Chemnitz University, BirdNET uses deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to classify avian acoustic events.

It treats audio spectrograms as images, recognizing visual patterns in sound frequencies similar to how humans identify objects in photos. Our implementation runs this sophisticated model locally on the server.

6,000+ Species
98.4% mAP Score
2

The Pipeline

01
Input Acquisition

Accepts raw .wav audio directly from ESP32 edge devices or manual user uploads.

02
Spectrogram Conversion

Audio is transformed into a Mel Spectrogram—a visual representation of frequency over time that the AI can "see".

03
CNN Processing

The spectrogram image is processed by a Residual MobileNet architecture to extract distinct acoustic features.

04
Classification Output

Returns a structured JSON list of species names and confidence scores (e.g., "Northern Cardinal: 0.85").
